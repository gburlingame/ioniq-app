The diagnostic suite

Every signal the car will share, made legible

Connect an inexpensive ELM327 Bluetooth adapter and read your vehicle's live data — cleanly organized, charted, and recorded.

Live Dashboard

State of charge, available energy, cell voltages, pack temperatures, 12V, motor RPM, regen, tire pressures — a full live readout you can reorder to taste.

Charging Telemetry

Live AC and DC charging detection, EVSE max voltage / current / power, Control Pilot duty cycle, and ICCU input voltage and current.

Charging intelligence

Why is my charge slow?

Compare what your vehicle asked for against what the charger actually delivered — and what the EVSE advertised it could do. Spot a bottlenecking charger instead of guessing.

Reach 80% sooner

Save time

A preconditioned battery accepts more power, so it climbs to 80% faster. This live ETA shows exactly when your pack reaches its ideal fast-charge temperature — so you time your arrival, charge at full speed, and spend less time at the plug.

  • check_circleOn the CarPlay Driving tab too — track it the whole drive to the station
